Projects

LearnScramble

Workplace vocabulary for people working in a new language — iOS, live on the App Store.

App Store Website How it works

Pick your job (32 professions) and the language you need: spell the words you really use at work from scrambled letters, hear them, say them out loud and pick them by ear. Point the camera at a tool and the app tells you its name in both languages. I built it alone, end to end.

  • SwiftUI app with SwiftData progress, a pronunciation test (Speech framework), a listening test and a camera tool scanner (AVFoundation)
  • 54 interface languages, right-to-left included, and separate layouts for regular iPhones and the folding iPhone
  • Server in TypeScript on Firebase Cloud Functions: words and pictures made with OpenAI and stored in a shared Firestore word bank, so the AI key never ships in the app
  • Security and cost control: App Check, anonymous sign-in, per-user daily and monthly limits, alert emails
  • Monetisation: one-time profession unlocks with StoreKit 2, free trial words counted in the Keychain
  • Shipped properly: TestFlight, App Review, privacy manifest, privacy policy (Canada / Québec Law 25), Crashlytics and opt-in Analytics
