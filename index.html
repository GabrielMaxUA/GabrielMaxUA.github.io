01 · Featured work

LearnScramble

Workplace vocabulary for people starting a job in a new language. iOS app, live on the App Store.

View on the App Store Website
  • Spell it
  • Track progress
  • Scan a tool
  • Pick by ear

What it does

You pick your job (32 professions) and the language you need. Then you spell the words you'll actually use at work from scrambled letters, hear them, say them out loud and pick them by ear. Point the camera at a tool and the app names it in both languages.

The source code is private (it's a commercial app). I'm happy to walk you through the codebase on a screen share during an interview.

◆iOS app

SwiftUI + SwiftData

  • 15,000+ lines of Swift: MVVM with SwiftUI, @Observable view models and async/await
  • SwiftData for progress, offline-first
  • Speech framework pronunciation test, AVFoundation camera scanner
  • 54 languages including right-to-left Arabic, Hebrew and Farsi, adaptive layouts for iPhone, iPad and foldable screens

◆Backend

0 API keys in the app

  • Firebase Cloud Functions in TypeScript
  • Words and pictures generated with OpenAI and stored in a shared Firestore word bank
  • API keys stay server-side in Secret Manager and never ship in the app

◆Security & cost

Per-user limits

  • App Check (App Attest) on every call, plus anonymous sign-in
  • Per-user daily and monthly limits
  • Alert emails when usage spikes
